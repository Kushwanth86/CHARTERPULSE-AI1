# CHARTERPULSE AI --- Risk Engine

## Risk categories

-   freight volatility
-   fuel/bunker movement
-   port congestion
-   vessel delay
-   weather disruption
-   operational delay
-   demand/inventory risk
-   geopolitical disruption where relevant

## Methods

### Sensitivity

Vary selected inputs and measure decision impact.

### Stress testing

Apply defined freight, fuel, congestion and weather shocks.

### Monte Carlo

Sample uncertain variables from validated distributions when enough data
exists.

## Outputs

Expected cost, range, downside scenario, statistically justified
probability/risk indicator and recommendation stability.

## What-if simulator

User-adjustable freight, fuel, congestion, weather and demand
assumptions can trigger recalculation.

## Rule

Risk scores must be derived from documented inputs. Never hard-code an
unsupported percentage.
